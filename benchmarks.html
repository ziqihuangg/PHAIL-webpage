Evaluation metrics

MetricUsed inMeasuresLimitation
Binary success rateMeta-World, RLBench, LIBERO, RoboTwin, ManiSkill3Did the task complete?Coarse - hides near-misses, smoothness, safety
Chain / sequential success rateCALVINAverage consecutive subtasks completed (0-5)Only for explicitly chained task designs
Partial-completion / progress scoreRoboChallenge, BEHAVIOR-1K, RoboDojoCredit for partial progress, not just pass/failScoring rubric differs per benchmark
Success-rate drop under perturbationThe ColosseumRobustness across 14 environment-perturbation axesMeasures robustness, not raw capability
Sim-real correlation (Pearson / MMRV)SIMPLERWhether sim ranking predicts real-robot rankingDoes not score capability directly
Pairwise preference / EloRoboArenaHead-to-head human preference across policiesExpensive, hard to scale, no absolute success number
Lifelong-learning transfer (FWT / NBT / AUC)LIBEROTransfer over a task sequenceSpecific to continual-learning setups
Integration costXPolicyLabTime to plug a new policy into an evaluation harnessInfrastructure metric, not model quality
Throughput (units/hour) + MTBFPhAILIndustrial productivity and reliabilityEconomic metrics remain rare in real-world evaluation

Not yet standardized: cost per successful rollout, longitudinal reliability drift, contamination auditing, safety incidents, and robustness to ambiguous instructions.
