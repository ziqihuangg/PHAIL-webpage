Model spotlight

GPT-6-Astra as an embodied policy

gpt-6-astra is OpenAI's general flagship model, with no robotics-specific training. Everyone below is prompting it through their own harness and reporting what happened - four labs, four different task sets and robots, none of it averaged into one score.

Naming note: this “Astra” is OpenAI's. Unrelated to Google DeepMind's Project Astra, which this site tracks separately on the Tasks page as a software-only agent.

At a glance

Harness, role, and effort level per source

RoboDojo defines its own three-level scale for "how the LLM touches the robot," and we reuse that vocabulary below since it's the clearest way to say what's actually comparable. L1: a trained policy acts directly, no LLM. L2 / Hybrid: the LLM directs a separately-trained policy. L3 / Direct: the LLM's own output drives the robot through a fixed, non-learned harness - no trained checkpoint anywhere in the loop. Where a source doesn't state its level or reasoning effort, we mark it "not stated" rather than guess.

Independent / draft

RoboLab cross-check

Unpublished draft report

An anonymized report titled "GPT 6 Astra as an Embodied Policy" (draft, 2026-09-13 - reads like a double-blind review companion site) re-ran GPT-6-Astra on RoboLab, a different sim benchmark from RoboDojo. It says explicitly that its 10-task, 5-trial selection is not a full RoboLab-120 reproduction, and three of five methods below are reused historical baselines, not freshly run.

MethodSuccess rate
GPT-6-Astra, direct (L3)98% (49/50)
π0.5 + GPT-6-Astra (L2 / hybrid)92% (46/50)
π0.5 alone (historical)36% (18/50)
Cosmos3-Nano-Policy (historical)36% (18/50)
DreamZero (historical)34% (17/50)

Gap is largest on tasks the π0.5 baseline fails outright, smallest on tasks everything already handles (e.g. Rubik's cube). Per-task breakdown and token/compute-cost figures are in the source.

anonymous-report-421.github.io/public-website - unpublished/draft, treat accordingly.

Small-scale study

GPT-Policy-Eval

In-context learning from video demonstrations

An independent framework testing whether showing GPT-6-Astra a demonstration (human video, robot video, robot video + action labels) improves real-robot task success, n=3 trials per condition. Two of its task families, in full:

TaskContextSuccessDecisionsTime (min)

"Decisions" is the number of model decision-steps in the run, not a dollar cost. Plug-reinsertion and goal-image tasks follow the same pattern in the full results gallery.
