Ranking

Ranks count every fitted model, so a filtered view keeps the original numbers. Each model's best entry on each board is used.

Index method

  1. Six boards, each run by its operatorRoboDojo Sim and Real, RoboChallenge T30 and T30-v2, RoboTwin 2.0, RoboArena. Paper tables join only when switched on, at half weight.
  2. Never add scores across boardsA board only says who beat whom. 34 on RoboDojo and 64 on RoboChallenge are different scales.
  3. Small gaps count as tiesP(A beats B) = Φ(gap / noise), with noise from the board's trial count. A 1-point gap over 180 real trials is only about 60/40.
  4. Harder boards count moreWeight = 1 − best score / 100: LIBERO (best 97%) ≈ 0, RoboDojo Sim (best 34%) ≈ 0.66. One operator's boards share one weight.
  5. Big boards don't outvote small onesEach model receives a board's weight once, however many rivals share that board.
  6. Pool with Bradley-TerryOne strength per model from every pairwise result - the maths behind Chatbot Arena.
  7. Index = expected win rate0-100: the share of head-to-heads a model should win against every other ranked model.
  8. Show the doubtWhisker: lowest and highest index when any one board is dropped. Boards column: how many boards a model rests on.

For comparison only, on the Method switch: Percentile mean ignores how strong each board's field is; Raw mean is the plain weighted sum of published scores.

Boards and weights

  • Weight = difficulty × family share × evidence.
  • Held-out accuracy: fit without the board, then check its clearly separated pairs. 50% is a coin flip; red is below 60%.

Capabilities, decoupled

  • The same fit, run once per capability.
  • Five of the seven columns rest on RoboDojo alone; · means never measured.

Model gaps

  • Where models on the benchmark-run boards still fail, read off the raw published scores, not the index.
  • Counted from the ledger each time the page loads. “What is tested” quotes the board's own definition.

Board agreement

  • Kendall's τ over the models two boards share (four or more).
  • +1 same order, −1 reversed. Red cells: the boards disagree.

Index against model size

  • No board publishes latency, control rate or hardware.
  • Parameter count is the only proxy, and most models do not state it.
